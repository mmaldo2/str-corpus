### Zoning-era OOC (Bassett)
Bianchi v. Comm'r of Pub. Bldgs., 279 Mass. 136 (1932); Osborn v. Town of
Darien, 119 Conn. 182 (1934); Downs v. Sea Bright, 12 N.J. Misc. 11 (1933);
Koch v. City of Toledo, 37 F.2d 336 (1930); De Lano v. City of Tulsa, 26 F.2d
640 (1928). Bassett black-letter (pp. 101, 195-96): boarders/roomers are a
proper ACCESSORY USE of a family residence in a residence district while
incidental to the family's own occupancy; a boarding/rooming house as MAIN
use is an excludable business.
