Right-to-Let Corpus Engine — how the machine builds the historical record

Cities are banning short-term rentals, and the strongest counter-arguments are historical: they turn on proving that letting rooms or a dwelling for short periods, for pay, has been an ordinary, lawful incident of property ownership for two centuries. The problem is that the old cases use vanished vocabulary no modern search reaches. This is an interactive map of the system recovering that record. Pick a numbered flow below, press Next to walk through it — and click any dotted term for a plain-English definition.
